Journey to Greece

Things to know about Greece

Greece is an european country known for its ancient history, thousands of islands, and its role as cradle of western civilization. Athens is the capital and largest city. Their official language is Greek, and citizens often call themselves Hellenes.

Ancient Greece produced figures like Socrates,plato, and Aristotle city-states such as Athens and Sparta influenced global political and cultural development.

Greece has a population of 10.4 million people and over 2,000 islands and about 170 inhabited, including Crete, Rhodes and the Cyclades .

Climate in Greece

Greece has a mediterranean climate:
  • Hot, dry summers
  • Mild, wet winters
Northern mountains regions experiencecols winters and snowfalls

Things that entice tourists to visit Greece:

  1. Ancient history, e.g. The Acropolis, The Temple os Zeus, Ancient Delphi, The Palace of Knossos.
  2. Beautiful islands e.g Santorini,Mykonos, Crete, Corfu.
Crystal-clear beaches such as : Navagio Beach, Elafonisi Beach, Balos Lagoon. Mediterranean Food Culture such as: Fresh seafood, local tavernas, olive oil tasting, wine tours.
